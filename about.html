How KFPL Started

Our Story

KFPL was created by Alvar and Tevin as a project inspired by our experience at Moringa School.

As football fans and students learning technology, we wanted to combine our interests and create something that Kenyan football fans could enjoy.

Our idea was to create a Kenyan-focused fantasy football experience where fans could build teams, follow players, compete on leaderboards, and enjoy the Premier League in a different way.
